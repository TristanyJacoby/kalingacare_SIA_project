Our Story

From a Stockroom in Cubao to Families Nationwide

KalingaCare started with a problem many OFW families know well: money sent home for a parent's medicine doesn't always reach its purpose.

  1. 2022

    The Idea

    A small group of children of OFWs in Quezon City kept hearing the same story: remittances meant for checkups and medicine were spent elsewhere, and parents went without. They asked a simple question: what if OFWs could buy the care itself?

  2. 2023

    The First Deliveries

    KalingaCare began as a social media page taking orders from OFWs in the Middle East, packing walkers and blood pressure monitors from a small stockroom in Cubao and delivering them across Metro Manila.

  3. 2024

    Reaching Every Province

    Courier partnerships took deliveries beyond Metro Manila to families anywhere in the Philippines, with Cash on Delivery so loved ones could see the item before paying.

  4. 2025

    The Online Store

    The KalingaCare website opened, letting OFWs browse, order and track deliveries in one place, with prices shown in the currency of the country they work in.

  5. 2026

    The App and Online Payments

    The KalingaCare Android app launched alongside card, GCash and Maya payments and Kali, our chat assistant. Today we serve 500+ OFW families, and we're still guided by the same idea: care beyond borders.
