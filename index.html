About KalingaCare

Caregiving Doesn't Need a Plane Ticket

Millions of OFWs send money home for their parents' medical needs, but it doesn't always reach its purpose. KalingaCare lets you buy healthcare products directly for your loved ones — a "remittance-in-kind" that goes straight to what matters, not through anyone else's hands.

  • Curated senior wellness & medical products
  • Delivered directly to your family in the Philippines
